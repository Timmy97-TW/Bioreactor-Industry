Executive summary

Eight findings八項發現

Written for a team building a small perfusion bioreactor and deciding what it may claim. Every figure is attributed. Where the sources disagree, the disagreement is the finding.寫給一支正在做小型灌流生物反應器、並且要決定自己能主張什麼的團隊。所有數字都附出處。當來源彼此矛盾時，矛盾本身就是發現。

1 · The market's size is unknown to a factor of four市場規模的不確定性達四倍

Nine publishers price the 2025 bioreactor market between $6.09 bn and $25.9 bn. None discloses a methodology. Company filings put a floor under the range: Sartorius Bioprocess Solutions alone was €2,865 m in 2025.九家出版商對 2025 年生物反應器市場的估價介於 60.9 億與 259 億美元之間，沒有一家揭露方法論。公司財報為區間畫出下限：光是 Sartorius 的 Bioprocess Solutions，2025 年就有 28.65 億歐元。

2 · The shortage is of large vessels, not of vessels缺的是大槽，不是槽

Fewer than one fermentation facility in five has a vessel above 20,000 L and only nine in the world exceed 100,000 L, while more than half of surveyed companies want larger capacity.不到五分之一的發酵廠擁有 20,000 L 以上的槽體，全球只有九座超過 100,000 L，而超過半數受訪公司想要更大的產能。

3 · The downturn is over, but equipment lags衰退結束了，但設備落後

Sartorius orders turned in Q4 2024, a year before revenue. In the first half of 2026 consumables grew about 9 % and equipment about 2 %. Two vendors are still contracting, and Donaldson wrote off $62.0 m of bioreactor acquisitions.Sartorius 的訂單在 2024 年第四季反轉，比營收早一年。2026 年上半年耗材成長約 9%，設備約 2%。有兩家供應商仍在萎縮，而 Donaldson 對其生物反應器併購認列了 6,200 萬美元減損。

4 · The vessel is a razor槽體是刮鬍刀

Between 2021 and 2026 the disclosed acquisition money went to filtration, transfection reagents, biopreservation media, resins and analytics. The only two purchases of actual bioreactor companies were the smallest, and both were impaired.2021 至 2026 年，揭露的併購金額流向過濾、轉染試劑、生物保存介質、層析介質與分析。唯二真正買下生物反應器公司的交易金額最小，而且兩筆都已減損。

5 · Intensification is settled science製程強化已成定論

N-1 perfusion roughly doubles titre, shown independently across four and six CHO cell lines and scaled to 500 L. Perfusion runs about five times the volumetric productivity of fed-batch. 42.3 % of facilities were evaluating it in 2026 and only 11.4 % were not considering it.N-1 灌流使效價約倍增，在四株與六株 CHO 細胞上各自獨立證實，並放大到 500 L。灌流的體積生產力約為饋料批次的五倍。2026 年有 42.3% 的廠房正在評估，完全不考慮的只剩 11.4%。

6 · The single-use environmental case just reversed拋棄式的環境論證剛剛翻轉

A 2026 bottom-up analysis of real 2,000 L facilities found the carbon footprint of single-use significantly higher than assumed, with stainless steel competitive as grids decarbonise. It contradicts the 2013 and 2017 studies the industry has cited for a decade.2026 年一份針對真實 2,000 L 廠房的由下而上分析發現，拋棄式的碳足跡顯著高於先前假設，而隨著電網去碳化，不鏽鋼具競爭力。這與產業引用了十年的 2013 與 2017 年研究相反。

7 · Throughput is not the constraint. Approval is.限制不是產能，是核准。

Cellares raised $327 m by June 2026 on a claim of ten times the batches of a conventional facility, and in August Bristol Myers Squibb ended a $380 m agreement because the system could not meet the requirements for commercial batches of an approved product. 168 US roles were cut.Cellares 到 2026 年 6 月募得 3.27 億美元，主張批次產出為傳統廠房的十倍；8 月 Bristol Myers Squibb 終止 3.8 億美元的協議，理由是該系統無法滿足一項已核准產品商業批次的要求。美國裁員 168 人。

8 · On-farm production exists, and its quality data is alarming農場端生產是存在的，而它的品質資料令人不安

Embrapa sampled eighteen on-farm inoculants: all eighteen were contaminated and none of the target organism was recovered from six of them.巴西於 2024 年 12 月將農場端生物投入品生產合法化，如今已觸及四分之一的種植面積。Embrapa 取樣十八份農場自產接種劑：十八份全部受污染，其中六份完全沒有分離出目標菌。

01 · The market

Nobody agrees how big this industry is沒有人對這個產業有多大達成共識

Nine published estimates of the 2025 bioreactor market span a factor of 4.3. That is not sloppiness. It is a disagreement about what a bioreactor is: the vessel alone, the vessel plus its single-use consumables, or the whole fermenter category. Read the range, and read what each publisher counted.九份公開報告對 2025 年生物反應器市場的估計相差 4.3 倍。這不是粗心，而是對「生物反應器是什麼」的定義分歧：只算槽體、算槽體加拋棄式耗材、還是整個發酵設備類別。要看的是區間，以及每家算了什麼。

Hardware is the small half硬體是比較小的那一半

Wider single-use bioprocessing, meaning bags, filters, tubing and sensors, is put at $16.5 bn in 2024 growing to $33.7 bn by 2030. The consumable stream is larger than the machine that consumes it.更廣義的拋棄式生物製程，包含袋體、濾器、管路與感測器，2024 年估計為 165 億美元，2030 年成長到 337 億美元。耗材流比消耗它的機器還大。

Stainless has not lost不鏽鋼並沒有輸

Stainless steel still takes about 47 % of 2025 bioreactor revenue. Over 90 % of surveyed facilities use single-use somewhere, which is a penetration figure and is routinely misquoted as a volume share. The two are not the same claim.不鏽鋼仍占 2025 年生物反應器營收約 47%。超過 90% 的受訪廠房「在某處」使用拋棄式，這是滲透率，卻經常被誤引為體積占比。兩者不是同一個主張。

The disagreement is structural分歧是結構性的

On single-use bioreactor growth, Roots Analysis says 9.0 % a year and Precedence says 18.3 %. One of those describes a maturing category and the other a compounding one. No public methodology exists to adjudicate.對於拋棄式生物反應器的成長率，Roots Analysis 說每年 9.0%，Precedence 說 18.3%。一個描述成熟市場，一個描述持續複利的市場。沒有公開方法論可供裁決。

A caution about the numbers on this page. Every market-size figure above comes from a commercial market-report landing page. None publishes a methodology, a sample, or a definition of the addressable base. Three aggregator sites that surfaced repeatedly with confident dollar-per-litre figures, and one widely repeated "25 million litres of global precision fermentation capacity" claim, could not be traced to any primary document and have been left out. Company filings, the BioPlan and bioTRAK surveys, the Capacitor database and the peer-reviewed techno-economic papers are the load-bearing sources here.關於本頁數字的提醒。上述所有市場規模數字均來自商業市調報告頁面，皆未公開方法論、樣本或可計算基數的定義。有三個聚合網站反覆給出看似明確的每公升價格，以及一則流傳甚廣的「全球精準發酵產能約 2,500 萬公升」說法，都無法追溯到任何一手文件，已一律排除。真正承重的來源是公司財報、BioPlan 與 bioTRAK 調查、Capacitor 資料庫，以及同儕審查的技術經濟論文。
02 · The cycle

The industry has just come through its first real downturn這個產業剛剛走過它第一次真正的衰退

Customers over-ordered through the pandemic, then stopped buying while they worked through the stock. Sartorius is the clearest window on it, because it reports order intake separately from sales, and the two moved a year apart.客戶在疫情期間超額下單，接著在消化庫存的期間停止採購。Sartorius 是觀察這件事最清楚的窗口，因為它把訂單與銷售分開揭露，而這兩者相差了一年。

Recovered已復甦

Merck's Process Solutions went from organic minus 6.4 % in 2024 to plus 10.7 % in 2025 and plus 14.7 % in the second quarter of 2026. Repligen went from $632 m in 2023 and $634 m in 2024 to $738 m in 2025, and guides $813 to $835 m for 2026.Merck 的 Process Solutions 從 2024 年有機衰退 6.4%，變成 2025 年成長 10.7%、2026 年第二季成長 14.7%。Repligen 從 2023 年 6.32 億、2024 年 6.34 億美元，到 2025 年 7.38 億美元，2026 年指引 8.13 至 8.35 億美元。

Still falling仍在下滑

Eppendorf's bioprocess unit fell 25.6 % in 2024 to €88.1 m and a further 11.5 % in 2025 to €78.0 m, now about eight per cent of group revenue. Getinge's Life Science order intake fell 10.9 % in the fourth quarter of 2025, the only bioprocess-exposed order book to turn negative going into 2026.Eppendorf 的生物製程部門 2024 年下滑 25.6% 至 8,810 萬歐元，2025 年再跌 11.5% 至 7,800 萬歐元，現在約占集團營收 8%。Getinge 的 Life Science 訂單在 2025 年第四季下滑 10.9%，是唯一在進入 2026 年時訂單轉負的生物製程相關業務。

Permanently written down已永久減損

Donaldson bought its way into bioprocessing with roughly $275 m of tuck-ins, then took a $62.0 m impairment in its financial year to July 2025: $46.6 m against Univercells Technologies and $15.4 m against Solaris Biotech, citing weaker bioprocessing capital spending. The downturn's permanent damage landed on the sub-scale entrants, not on the incumbents.Donaldson 以約 2.75 億美元的併購進入生物製程，接著在 2025 年 7 月止的財年認列 6,200 萬美元減損：Univercells Technologies 4,660 萬美元、Solaris Biotech 1,540 萬美元，理由是生物製程資本支出疲弱。這波衰退的永久性傷害落在規模不足的新進者身上，不是在既有巨頭身上。

04 · Technology

Four technologies, at four very different stages四項技術，四個完全不同的階段

Process intensification has stopped being argued about. Single-use has just had its central environmental claim reversed. Process analytics has split into what is deployed and what is demonstrated. Digital twins are roughly a decade behind their own marketing.製程強化已經沒有人在爭論。拋棄式技術的核心環境主張剛剛被推翻。製程分析技術分裂成「已部署」與「只做過展示」兩塊。數位孿生大約落後自己的行銷語言十年。

Amgen's Singapore plant, the reference case for intensified manufacturing, occupies 120,000 square feet, which the company puts at 16 % the size of a conventional facility, built in half the construction time at approximately one quarter of the capital cost.Amgen 的新加坡廠是製程強化的標竿案例，占地 12 萬平方英尺，公司稱其為傳統廠房的 16%，建造時間減半，資本支出約為四分之一。

Permittivity probes track viable cell density with R² between 0.936 and 0.995 up to 130 million cells per mL, and using them to drive cell-specific perfusion rate cut media consumption by 24 to 26 % against volumetric control. That is infrastructure, not a demonstration. Raman models built on 1 L and 5 L vessels have been validated on a 2,000 L manufacturing bioreactor, with glucose error of 1.44 mM.介電常數探針追蹤活細胞密度，在每毫升 1.3 億顆以下 R² 介於 0.936 至 0.995；用它驅動細胞比灌流速率，相較於體積式控制可減少 24 至 26% 的培養基消耗。那是基礎設施，不是展示。以 1 L 與 5 L 建立的拉曼模型，已在 2,000 L 生產級反應器上獲得驗證，葡萄糖誤差 1.44 mM。

The FDA's operative process analytical technology framework is still the guidance finalised in October 2004.FDA 現行有效的製程分析技術架構，仍是 2004 年 10 月定稿的那份指引。

Not deployed: the digital twin尚未部署：數位孿生

A 2025 practitioner review offers no example of a digital twin running at commercial scale, names no regulatory approval pathway, and concedes that mechanistic models drift when parameters are unspecified while data-driven models fail outside their training domain. The FDA's January 2025 draft guidance on artificial intelligence is a risk-based credibility framework for a stated context of use. It is not an approval route.2025 年一篇從業者回顧，找不出任何一個在商業規模運行的數位孿生案例，說不出監管核准途徑，並承認機制模型在參數未指定時會偏移，而資料驅動模型在訓練域外會失效。FDA 2025 年 1 月關於人工智慧的指引草案，是針對特定使用情境的風險導向可信度架構，不是核准途徑。

The closest thing to autonomy actually in production is closed-loop feeding driven by capacitance. Control, not decision-making.真正在生產中、最接近自主的，是由電容量測驅動的閉迴路餵料。那是控制，不是決策。

The regulatory door for continuous processing is already open. ICH Q13 was adopted at step 4 on 16 November 2022, covers therapeutic proteins as well as chemical entities, and its Annex III describes exactly a perfusion bioreactor coupled to continuous capture chromatography. FDA adopted it in March 2023, EMA from July 2023. As of May 2026 the FDA reports seventeen drugs approved using continuous manufacturing and 191 applications accepted into its Emerging Technology Program, of which six are distributed or point-of-care manufacturing.連續式製程的監管大門已經打開。ICH Q13 於 2022 年 11 月 16 日在第四階段通過，涵蓋治療性蛋白與化學實體，其附錄三描述的正是灌流生物反應器串接連續式捕捉層析。FDA 於 2023 年 3 月採用，EMA 自 2023 年 7 月起適用。截至 2026 年 5 月，FDA 表示已有十七項藥品以連續式製造核准，新興技術計畫共受理 191 件申請，其中六件屬分散式或照護點製造。
05 · New entrants

The challengers, and what happened to them挑戰者，以及他們的下場

Between 2020 and 2022 a wave of companies set out to replace the stirred tank. Four years later the survivors have one thing in common, and it is not the reactor.2020 到 2022 年間，一批新創立志取代攪拌槽。四年後，活下來的公司有一個共同點，而那個共同點不是反應器。

The year's biggest reversal: Cellares今年最大的反轉：Cellares

Cellares closed a $257 M Series D on 28 January 2026, co-led by BlackRock and Eclipse, and grew it to $327 M on 15 June. Brazil's Lei 15.070/2024, signed on 23 December 2024, is the world's first framework that explicitly separates commercial biofactory production from on-farm production for a farmer's own use. Bioinputs covered 26 % of Brazil's planted area in the 2023/24 harvest, and over 70 million hectares received biological control products in 2022. Embrapa's three recommendations, taken together, read as a design specification for any credible farm-side reactor: strains only from official collections, mandatory registration of production units for traceability, and a qualified technical professional overseeing production. Two further facts change the shape of the argument. First, no commercial precedent exists anywhere for on-farm production of an engineered microbe or a recombinant peptide. Containment is cellular, not molecular. A 0.2 µm pore retains whole cells and passes 20 to 200 nm outer-membrane vesicles. The two experiments that change the tier能改變層級的兩個實驗

Against the five-tier hardware ladder, ReLeaf is characterised but not measured. 09 · Writing

What to put on the bioreactor page, and what to put on the homepage生物反應器頁面要寫什麼，首頁又要放什麼

The bioreactor page carries the argument. The homepage carries one sentence of it. They are not the same job, and the homepage sentence is the harder one.生物反應器頁面承載整段論證，首頁只承載其中一句。這是兩件不同的工作，而首頁那一句比較難。

The bioreactor page, in the order a judge reads it生物反應器頁面，依評審閱讀順序

  1. Open on the industry, not on ourselves. Fewer than one fermentation facility in five worldwide has a vessel above 20,000 L, and only nine have one above 100,000 L. The industry's shortage is of large vessels. Ours is a different shortage, and naming theirs first is what earns the right to name ours.從產業開場，不要從自己開場。全世界不到五分之一的發酵廠有 20,000 L 以上的槽體，只有九座超過 100,000 L。產業缺的是大槽。我們缺的是另一種東西，而先講清楚他們缺什麼，才換得到講我們缺什麼的資格。
  2. Then the architecture, with the reason it was chosen. Perfusion with full cell retention, single-use module, scale out by module count. Cite van Reis for the scale-up rule and say which similarity group is sacrificed. Dr. Yuet recommended a packed bed and we still owe him a written justification for hollow fibre. Put that on the page. It reads as engineering, not as an oversight.再講架構，並且講為什麼選它。全細胞截留的灌流、拋棄式模組、以模組數量橫向擴充。放大規則引用 van Reis，並說明放棄了哪一組相似性。Yuet 老師建議的是填充床，我們還欠他一份選擇中空纖維的書面理由。把這件事寫在頁面上。它讀起來是工程，不是疏漏。
  3. Then the measurement, and the failure inside it. 434 hours of unbroken logging. The scatter rose between 240 and 300 hours, and over exactly those hours the photometer's reference channel fell from 25.8 to 9.3 lux. The instrument recorded when to distrust itself. Lead with that, not with the growth curve.再講量測，以及量測裡的失效。434 小時不間斷紀錄。散布在第 240 至 300 小時之間放大，而正好在那幾個小時，光度計的參考通道從 25.8 lux 掉到 9.3 lux。儀器記錄下了何時不該相信自己。用這件事開頭，不要用生長曲線。
  4. Then the model, with its corrections visible. The critical-flux particle radius was wrong at 1.5 µm and is right at 0.7 µm, and correcting it moved the fouling margin from 0.26 to 0.73. Showing a correction is worth more than showing a result.再講模型，並且讓修正可見。臨界通量的粒徑取 1.5 µm 是錯的，正確值是 0.7 µm，修正後結垢餘裕從 0.26 移到 0.73。展示一次修正，比展示一個結果值錢。
  5. Then the limits, at the same type size as the claims. Every invariant is a model output. No biological output has been measured. Delivery stops at a reservoir. The membrane cutoff is unchosen. Contained use is a classification being pursued, not one held.再講限制，字級與主張相同。所有不變量都是模型輸出。沒有量測到任何生物產出。輸送止於儲槽。膜截留值未選定。封閉使用是正在爭取的分類，不是已經持有的分類。

Source hierarchy來源層級

Three aggregator sites that repeatedly produced confident dollar-per-litre figures with no traceable provenance.
