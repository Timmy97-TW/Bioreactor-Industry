03 · The players

Five layers, not three, and the vessel is the least valuable one五層，不是三層，而槽體是其中最不值錢的一層

The usual framing splits the industry into equipment giants, hardware disruptors and cloud platforms. That misses the two layers where the money actually moved between 2022 and 2026.常見的分法把產業切成設備巨頭、硬體破壞者與雲端平台。這個分法漏掉了 2022 至 2026 年間，錢真正流動的那兩層。

Table 1. The industry in five layers. Revenue figures are the most recent disclosed.表 1。產業的五個層次。營收為最近一次揭露值。
Layer層次 Who誰 What they are genuinely best at他們真正擅長什麼
1 Platform integrators平台整合商 Sartorius, Danaher (Cytiva, Pall), Thermo Fisher, Merck KGaA Not the vessel. The filed process. Cytiva supports more than 90 % of global monoclonal antibody production volume, and its moat is chromatography media inside approved processes. Sartorius Bioprocess Solutions was €2,865 m in 2025, more than three quarters of group sales. Thermo's answer at scale is the 5,000 L DynaDrive with a 20 to 1 turndown.不是槽體，是已備查的製程。Cytiva 支撐全球九成以上的單株抗體生產量，護城河是「已核准製程」裡的層析介質。Sartorius 的 Bioprocess Solutions 2025 年營收 28.65 億歐元，超過集團的四分之三。Thermo 在大規模端的答案是 20 比 1 調節比的 5,000 L DynaDrive。
2 Specialist vessel makers槽體專業廠 Eppendorf, Getinge / Applikon, ABEC, PBS Biotech, INFORS HT, Bioengineering AG, Distek, Cellexus Geometry and range. ABEC sits at the extreme with a 6,000 L single-use bioreactor and, unusually, a non-proprietary film supply chain. PBS Biotech sells a vertical wheel from 0.1 L to 80 L for shear-sensitive cells. This is also the layer taking the most damage: Eppendorf's bioprocess revenue has fallen two years running.幾何形狀與量程。ABEC 位於極端，做到 6,000 L 拋棄式生物反應器，而且少見地採用非專屬的膜材供應鏈。PBS Biotech 賣的是 0.1 L 到 80 L 的垂直輪，用於剪切敏感細胞。這一層受傷也最重：Eppendorf 的生物製程營收已連續兩年下滑。
3 Intensification and component suppliers製程強化與零組件供應商 Repligen, Pall, Metenova, 908 Devices, sensor and film makers Selling the physics rather than the tank. Repligen's XCell ATF cell retention device is the reference part for perfusion and N-1 intensification, and the company grew 16 % in 2025 while vessel makers shrank. It is the purest listed bet on intensification.賣的是物理，不是桶槽。Repligen 的 XCell ATF 細胞截留裝置，是灌流與 N-1 製程強化的參考零件；2025 年這家公司成長 16%，而槽體廠在萎縮。它是上市公司中對製程強化最純粹的押注。
4 Capacity operators產能營運商 WuXi Biologics, Boehringer Ingelheim, Lonza, Liberation Bioindustries, Planetary, Cauldron Selling litres and batches, not steel. WuXi Biologics planned about 430,000 L of capacity from 2024 and runs the world's largest single-use line at 36,000 L. Its revenue grew 16.7 % in 2025 and 18.4 % in the first half of 2026, which is the plainest evidence that the BIOSECURE Act has not yet bitten commercially.賣的是公升數與批次，不是鋼。WuXi Biologics 規劃自 2024 年起約 43 萬公升產能，並運轉全球最大的 36,000 L 拋棄式產線。其 2025 年營收成長 16.7%、2026 年上半年成長 18.4%，這是 BIOSECURE 法案尚未在商業上咬到人的最直接證據。
5 Intelligence layer智慧層 Roebling (ex-Synonym), Ark Biotech, TeselaGen, n!Biomachines, Culture Biosciences Console Modelling, planning and control software. This layer grew by absorbing companies that failed to sell hardware. No incumbent has published a quantified digital or AI revenue line, so treat vendor claims here as marketing until a segment number appears.建模、規劃與控制軟體。這一層是靠吸收「賣不動硬體的公司」長大的。沒有任何一家既有大廠公布過數位或 AI 的量化營收，因此在看到分部數字之前，這裡的廠商說法都當作行銷看待。
China is gaining share in consumables first. Duoning Biotechnology, the best-documented Chinese case, went from RMB 193 m of revenue in 2020 to RMB 843 m in 2024, ranked fourth nationally in single-use products, and filed for a Hong Kong listing in January 2026. The domestically supplied share of China's bioprocess market rose from 19.2 % in 2017 to 26.4 % in 2021 on a market that grew from $1.0 bn to $5.1 bn. The pattern is bags and media first, vessels and control systems second.中國先在耗材上拿下份額。資料最完整的中國案例多寧生物，營收從 2020 年人民幣 1.93 億元成長到 2024 年 8.43 億元，在拋棄式產品排名全國第四，並於 2026 年 1 月申請香港上市。中國生物製程市場的國產供應占比，從 2017 年的 19.2% 上升到 2021 年的 26.4%，同期市場由 10 億美元成長到 51 億美元。順序是先袋體與培養基，再槽體與控制系統。
