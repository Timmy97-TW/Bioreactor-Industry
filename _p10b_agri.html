07 · The farm adjacency

Brazil already tried putting the reactor on the farm巴西已經試過把反應器放到農場上

This is the part of the map that a farm-side reactor actually has to answer to. There is one real precedent in the world, it is large, it is legal, and the published quality data from it is alarming.這是農場端反應器真正必須回答的那一塊地圖。世界上只有一個真實的先例，規模很大，合法，而且已發表的品質資料令人不安。

The market is real and the majors are barely in it市場是真的，而大廠幾乎不在裡面

Agricultural biologicals are put at $18.4 bn in 2025 growing to $35.0 bn by 2030, although estimates for the same year span $12.2 bn to $18.4 bn. What matters more is the share inside the majors: biologicals are 6.5 % of Corteva's crop protection revenue, 4 % of FMC's, 2.5 % of Syngenta's and 1.5 % of Bayer's, after a decade of acquisitions. The top eight biologicals companies hold only about 35 % of the category, against 38 % for the top three in seeds and crop protection.農業生物製劑 2025 年估計 184 億美元，2030 年成長到 350 億美元，不過同一年的估計值橫跨 122 億到 184 億美元。更重要的是它在大廠內部的占比：經過十年併購，生物製劑占 Corteva 作物保護營收 6.5%、FMC 4%、Syngenta 2.5%、Bayer 1.5%。前八大生物製劑公司只握有這個類別約 35%，而種子與作物保護的前三大握有 38%。

And the two biggest pure-play bets lost而兩個最大的純粹押注輸了

Indigo Ag peaked at a $3.5 bn valuation in 2020 and raised at about $200 m in 2023, a 94 % cut. AgBiome warned it might lay off all 123 staff in December 2023 despite a record sales year, having failed to raise; Ginkgo bought its platform assets in April 2024 at a fair value of $18.2 m. Joyn Bio, the Bayer and Ginkgo joint venture, was dissolved in 2022. Scale-up and distribution killed these companies, not biology.Indigo Ag 在 2020 年估值見頂 35 億美元，2023 年以約 2 億美元募資，跌幅 94%。AgBiome 在 2023 年 12 月警告可能資遣全部 123 名員工，儘管當年銷售創新高，原因是募不到資金；Ginkgo 於 2024 年 4 月以 1,820 萬美元的公允價值買下其平台資產。Bayer 與 Ginkgo 的合資公司 Joyn Bio 於 2022 年解散。殺死這些公司的是放大與通路，不是生物學。

The Brazilian precedent, and the study that should be on every farm-side reactor page巴西的先例，以及每一個農場端反應器頁面都該引用的那份研究

Brazil's Lei 15.070/2024, signed on 23 December 2024, is the world's first framework that explicitly separates commercial biofactory production from on-farm production for a farmer's own use. On-farm bioinputs are exempt from registration. Sale is prohibited. Strains must come from accredited germplasm collections, so multiplying a commercial product is not allowed. Bioinputs covered 26 % of Brazil's planted area in the 2023/24 harvest, and over 70 million hectares received biological control products in 2022. SoluBio, the closest thing to a commercial farm-side reactor business, serves more than two million hectares and over 400 customers on revenue above R$180 m a year. Its EBITDA margin fell from 69.6 % in 2022 to 1.7 % in 2023.巴西於 2024 年 12 月 23 日簽署的 Lei 15.070/2024，是全世界第一個把商業生物工廠生產與農民自用的農場端生產明確分開的框架。農場端生物投入品免登記，但禁止銷售。菌株必須來自認可的種質保存中心，因此不得直接繁殖商業產品。2023/24 年期，生物投入品覆蓋巴西 26% 的種植面積；2022 年有超過 7,000 萬公頃使用生物防治產品。最接近「農場端反應器事業」的 SoluBio，服務超過 200 萬公頃、400 多個客戶，年營收超過 1.8 億雷亞爾。其 EBITDA 利潤率從 2022 年的 69.6% 降到 2023 年的 1.7%。

Embrapa sampled eighteen on-farm inoculants across five Brazilian states. All eighteen were contaminated, and the commercial controls contained none. No Bradyrhizobium at all was recovered from the six samples that were supposed to contain it. Of 84 contaminant isolates across 25 genera, 44 % of the genera harbour species potentially pathogenic to humans, and 18 of 34 such isolates showed antibiotic resistance that was not intrinsic.Embrapa 在巴西五個州取樣了十八份農場自產接種劑。十八份全數受到污染，商業對照組則完全沒有。原本應含 Bradyrhizobium 的六份樣品，完全沒有分離出該菌。84 株污染菌分屬 25 個屬，其中 44% 的屬含有可能對人類致病的種，而 34 株相關分離株中有 18 株表現出非固有的抗生素抗性。

Embrapa's three recommendations, taken together, read as a design specification for any credible farm-side reactor: strains only from official collections, mandatory registration of production units for traceability, and a qualified technical professional overseeing production. Embrapa also warns that the practice creates structural inequality, because large farms can hire that expertise and smallholders cannot. That is the sharpest objection a judge can put to a farm-side device, and it deserves an answer rather than a slogan.Embrapa 的三項建議合起來，其實就是任何一台可信的農場端反應器的設計規格：菌株只能來自官方保存中心、生產單位必須登記以利追溯、生產須有合格技術人員監督。Embrapa 同時警告，這種做法會製造結構性不平等，因為大農場請得起那位專業人員，小農請不起。這是評審能對農場端裝置提出的最尖銳質疑，它需要的是回答，不是口號。

Two further facts change the shape of the argument. First, no commercial precedent exists anywhere for on-farm production of an engineered microbe or a recombinant peptide. Brazil's movement is multiplication of wild-type strains only, and an engineered organism there would fall under biosafety law rather than the on-farm exemption. Second, and more usefully, Ginkgo's agricultural lead states that metabolites and peptides derived from engineered microbes face easier approval than the microbes themselves, because the organism does not enter the market. A device whose output is a protein rather than a live cell is arguing on the easier side of that line, provided it can show the cells stay in.還有兩件事會改變論證的形狀。第一，全世界沒有任何「在農場生產工程菌或重組胜肽」的商業先例。巴西的運動只涉及野生型菌株的繁殖，而工程菌在當地會落入生物安全法規，不適用農場端豁免。第二，也更有用的是：Ginkgo 的農業負責人指出，工程菌衍生的代謝物與胜肽，其核准難度低於菌本身，因為生物體不進入市場。一台輸出蛋白質而非活細胞的裝置，站在這條界線比較容易的那一側，前提是它能證明細胞留在裡面。

For Taiwan specifically, the Plant Variety and Plant Seed Act bars domestic promotion or sale of a transgenic plant without an approved field trial, with penalties of NT$1,000,000 to 5,000,000. No Taiwanese instrument specific to on-farm release of an engineered microbe was located. That absence is not permission, and it should be written as an open question rather than as a clear path.就台灣而言，《植物品種及種苗法》規定，未經核准田間試驗不得在國內推廣或銷售基因轉殖植物，罰則為新臺幣 100 萬至 500 萬元。目前未找到專門針對「工程微生物於農場端釋出」的台灣法規。這個空白不等於許可，應該寫成一個尚未解決的問題，而不是一條清楚的路。
