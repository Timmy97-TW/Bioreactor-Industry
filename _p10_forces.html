06 · To 2035

Regulation became the fast-moving variable監管變成了跑得最快的那個變數

For twenty years the slow part of biomanufacturing was the rulebook. Between December 2025 and July 2026 four governments moved at once, and the direction of all four is the same: build capacity at home, and make it easier to build it in more places.過去二十年，生物製造裡跑得最慢的是規則書。2025 年 12 月到 2026 年 7 月之間，四個政府同時動了，而四者的方向一致：把產能建在自己國內，並且讓「建在更多地方」變得更容易。

China's share is the number that moved everything推動一切的那個數字是中國的占比

China accounts for over 70 % of world bio-fermentation product output, on a domestic market of about RMB 1.1 trillion. The NSCEB report puts 79 % of US pharmaceutical companies as dependent on Chinese contract manufacturers.中國占全球生物發酵產品產量七成以上，內需市場約人民幣 1.1 兆元。NSCEB 報告指出，79% 的美國製藥公司依賴中國委託製造商。

Point of need is now a funded programme「所需之處」現在是有預算的計畫

BioMADE received $87 M from the Department of Defense in 2021 and $450 M in 2023, with a further $21.4 M across fourteen projects in April 2026. Its Maple Grove demonstration facility, 122,000 square feet with two 25,000 L fermenters, is funded at $132 M or more and due in 2028.BioMADE 於 2021 年獲美國國防部 8,700 萬美元、2023 年 4.5 億美元，2026 年 4 月再獲 2,140 萬美元分配於十四項專案。其 Maple Grove 示範廠占地 12.2 萬平方英尺，兩座 25,000 L 發酵槽，資金 1.32 億美元以上，預計 2028 年完工。

Point of care is clinically demonstrated照護點製造已有臨床實證

An academic centre in Thailand manufactured anti-CD19 CAR-T on a closed benchtop platform with nine of nine products made successfully, typically in twelve days, against three to five weeks for centralised commercial manufacture.泰國一家學術中心以封閉式桌上型平台製造抗 CD19 CAR-T，九件產品全數製造成功，通常十二天完成，而集中式商業製造需三到五週。
