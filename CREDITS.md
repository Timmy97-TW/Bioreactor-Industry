## Everything else

Every figure in `index.html` is original, drawn in SVG from data literals in
`_p90_script.html`. The underlying numbers are attributed in the report's source list to
the organisation that published them; those publications carry their own terms.

The vessel drawings in the scale figure are derived from published working volumes
assuming a 2:1 height to diameter ratio. They are indicative of scale and are not
drawings of any particular vendor's product.

No photographs are used. Where a photograph would have been the obvious illustration, a
measured or derived drawing is used instead, so that every picture on the page carries a
number that can be checked.
