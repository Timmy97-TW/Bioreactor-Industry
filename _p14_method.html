How this was built, and what it cannot tell you這份報告怎麼做出來的，以及它無法告訴你什麼

Source hierarchy來源層級

Load-bearing claims come from company filings and investor releases, peer-reviewed papers, regulator and government documents, and named industry surveys. Commercial market-report landing pages are used only for market sizing, and always as a range with the publisher named, because none of them discloses a methodology.承重的主張來自公司財報與投資人公告、同儕審查論文、監管機關與政府文件，以及具名的產業調查。商業市調報告頁面只用於市場規模，且一律以區間呈現並註明出版者，因為它們都沒有揭露方法論。

Vendor performance claims are labelled as vendor claims wherever they appear. Sponsored content is named as sponsored.廠商的效能主張，在出現的每一處都標示為廠商說法。贊助內容一律註明為贊助。

Deliberately excluded刻意排除

Three aggregator sites that repeatedly produced confident dollar-per-litre figures with no traceable provenance. A widely repeated claim of about 25 million litres of global precision fermentation capacity, which could not be found in any Synonym or GFI primary document. An assertion that single-use is 98 % of capacity by volume, which contradicts the peer-reviewed figure of about 13 %. A reported buyout offer for LanzaTech that does not appear in the filings.三個反覆給出看似明確、卻無法追溯來源的每公升價格的聚合網站。一則流傳甚廣、稱全球精準發酵產能約 2,500 萬公升的說法，在 Synonym 或 GFI 的一手文件中都找不到。一項稱拋棄式占體積產能 98% 的主張，與同儕審查的約 13% 相矛盾。一則 LanzaTech 收購報價的傳聞，在申報文件中並不存在。

Known holes已知的洞

Current capacity utilisation percentages sit behind a paywall; the only fully specified figures found are from 2012. Single-use share of new capacity by installed litres is not published cleanly anywhere. New-unit list prices for Ambr, BioFlo and Biostat are quote-only. No defensible ceiling for what a farmer will pay per hectare was found. No published cost per kilogram compares photobioreactor and open-pond cyanobacterial biomass for agriculture. Several private companies publish no financials at all.目前的產能利用率數字位於付費牆之後；唯一能完整取得的規格化數字來自 2012 年。以裝機公升數計的拋棄式新產能占比，任何地方都沒有乾淨的公開資料。Ambr、BioFlo 與 Biostat 的新機定價一律報價制。找不到「農民每公頃願付上限」的可靠數字。沒有比較光生物反應器與開放池培養藍綠菌生質、用於農業的每公斤成本文獻。數家私人公司完全不公布財務數字。

Dates and currency日期與幣別

Research was carried out on 3 September 2026 and every status was checked against that date. Several companies in section 05 have press releases that read as current but are a year or more stale, and those are flagged individually. Currencies are as reported, not converted.研究於 2026 年 9 月 3 日進行，所有狀態均以該日期查核。第 05 節有數家公司的新聞稿讀起來像是最新，實際上已停滯一年以上，個別標註。幣別依原始揭露，未做換算。
