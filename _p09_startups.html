05 · New entrants

The challengers, and what happened to them挑戰者，以及他們的下場

Between 2020 and 2022 a wave of companies set out to replace the stirred tank. Four years later the survivors have one thing in common, and it is not the reactor.2020 到 2022 年間，一批新創立志取代攪拌槽。四年後，活下來的公司有一個共同點，而那個共同點不是反應器。

The year's biggest reversal: Cellares今年最大的反轉：Cellares

Cellares closed a $257 M Series D on 28 January 2026, co-led by BlackRock and Eclipse, and grew it to $327 M on 15 June. Its Cell Shuttle claimed up to ten times more batches than a conventional CDMO at comparable footprint and headcount. On about 26 August 2026 Bristol Myers Squibb ended the partnership, having concluded that Cell Shuttle could not meet the requirements for commercial batches of Breyanzi. The underlying 2024 agreement reserved $380 M of capacity. 168 US positions were cut, roughly 100 of them effective 20 October 2026.Cellares 於 2026 年 1 月 28 日完成 2.57 億美元 D 輪，由 BlackRock 與 Eclipse 共同領投，6 月 15 日擴大至 3.27 億美元。其 Cell Shuttle 宣稱在相同廠房面積與人力下，批次產出可達傳統 CDMO 的十倍。2026 年 8 月 26 日前後，Bristol Myers Squibb 終止合作，理由是 Cell Shuttle 無法滿足 Breyanzi 商業批次的要求。該筆 2024 年協議原本保留 3.8 億美元產能。美國裁員 168 人，其中約 100 人於 2026 年 10 月 20 日生效。

The lesson for any hardware challenger. The binding constraint is not throughput. It is process equivalence to a route that a regulator has already approved.對任何硬體挑戰者的啟示。真正的限制不是產能，而是與監管機關「已核准製程」的等價性。

The quiet correction: Culture Biosciences安靜的修正：Culture Biosciences

The company most often described as the AWS of biomanufacturing did not wind down. It stopped renting runs and started selling the box. Stratyx 250, launched 1 April 2025, is a 250 mL single-use cart-mounted reactor with cloud control, optionally running a Cytiva consumable derived from Xcellerex. A Series C closed on 16 December 2025 led by existing investors. About $107 M raised, about 73 staff in May 2026.這家最常被稱為「生物製造界 AWS」的公司並沒有收攤，而是停止出租跑批、改成賣機器。2025 年 4 月 1 日推出的 Stratyx 250 是 250 mL 拋棄式、推車式、雲端控制的反應器，可選用源自 Xcellerex 的 Cytiva 耗材。2025 年 12 月 16 日由既有投資人領投完成 C 輪。累計募資約 1.07 億美元，2026 年 5 月約 73 名員工。

Its own numbers for the box, all vendor stated: 16 % lower cost per run, 25 % faster timelines, 30 % better scale-up success, five-month payback. The service years became the training data.其自行公布的數據（皆為廠商說法）：每批成本降低 16%、時程加快 25%、放大成功率提升 30%、五個月回本。服務期累積的資料，成了產品的訓練資料。

Hollow fibre, which is the architecture ReLeaf uses中空纖維，也就是 ReLeaf 採用的架構

The segment splits three ways, and the split is instructive. Where the product is a high-value protein or a viral vector, hollow fibre is commercially healthy. Where the product is food, the capital is grants and corporate strategics rather than venture.這個領域裂成三塊，而裂法本身很有啟發性。當產物是高價蛋白或病毒載體時，中空纖維在商業上很健康；當產物是食物時，資金來自補助與企業策略投資，而不是創投。

Repligen bought the supply chainRepligen 買下了供應鏈

Spectrum Life Sciences for about $371.5 m in 2017 brought KrosFlo hollow-fibre tangential flow and TFDF; Polymem for $47.0 m in 2021 brought in-house hollow-fibre membrane manufacturing. Its published perfusion numbers are 100 to 130 million viable cells per mL against 13 million in fed-batch, roughly ten times the product. Revenue grew 16 % in 2025 while vessel makers shrank.2017 年以約 3.715 億美元買下 Spectrum Life Sciences，取得 KrosFlo 中空纖維切向流與 TFDF；2021 年以 4,700 萬美元買下 Polymem，取得自有中空纖維膜製造。其公布的灌流數據是每毫升 1 億至 1.3 億顆活細胞，對比饋料批次的 1,300 萬顆，產物約十倍。2025 年營收成長 16%，同期槽體廠在萎縮。

FiberCell publishes a price listFiberCell 公布價目表

Unusual in this industry, and therefore the price anchor for the architecture. Cartridges run $526 to $937 across 80 cm² to 1.2 m², at a surface-area-to-volume ratio of 150 to 200 cm² per mL. The largest cartridge holds 5 × 10¹⁰ cells in 70 mL of extracapillary space and yields 5 to 20 mg of protein a day.這在業界少見，因此成為這個架構的價格錨點。膜柱從 80 cm² 到 1.2 m²，售價 526 至 937 美元，表面積對體積比為每毫升 150 至 200 cm²。最大的膜柱在 70 mL 的外毛細管腔中容納 5 × 10¹⁰ 顆細胞，每日產出 5 至 20 mg 蛋白質。

Cellular Agriculture Ltd is active, and scales the way ReLeaf doesCellular Agriculture Ltd 仍在營運，而且用和 ReLeaf 一樣的方式放大

Active at Companies House, accounts filed to April 2025. It moved in 2024 into a £3 m, 20,000 square foot site at Corsham Science Park and commissioned a pilot plant in 2026. It scales by fibre length rather than vessel volume, which is the same class of claim ReLeaf makes by module count. Funded by Innovate UK grants and a Hilton Foods equity stake; trackers disagree on the total raised, and the cell density behind its longest-run claim is not published.在英國公司登記處為存續狀態，已申報至 2025 年 4 月的財報。2024 年遷入 Corsham Science Park 一處 300 萬英鎊、2 萬平方英尺的場址，並於 2026 年啟用試量產廠。它以纖維長度而非槽體體積來放大，與 ReLeaf 以模組數量放大屬於同一類主張。資金來自 Innovate UK 補助與 Hilton Foods 的股權投資；各資料庫對募資總額說法不一，其「最長運轉」主張背後的細胞密度亦未公開。

The scale ReLeaf is actually atReLeaf 實際所在的尺度

Terumo BCT's Quantum Flex runs about 11,500 fibres at 2.1 m² with a lumen bore near 200 µm. A published run on that platform family reached 3.1 × 107 cells per mL and 2.5 × 1010 cells in 29 days, fifteen times static culture. ReLeaf's module is 11 fibres, 1.0 mm bore, 0.02 m². Same architecture, two orders of magnitude apart in area. That is worth stating plainly rather than leaving a judge to work out.Terumo BCT 的 Quantum Flex 使用約 11,500 根纖維、2.1 m²，內徑約 200 µm。同一平台家族的一份已發表運轉達到每毫升 3.1 × 107 顆細胞、29 天內 2.5 × 1010 顆，是靜置培養的十五倍。ReLeaf 的模組是 11 根纖維、內徑 1.0 mm、0.02 m²。架構相同，面積差兩個數量級。這件事值得直接寫出來，而不是留給評審自己算。

One more entrant is worth watching because it inverts the problem. EdiMembre, spun out of MilliporeSigma in August 2025 on a $400 K pre-seed, spins its hollow fibres from soy, mung bean and lentil protein isolate, so the scaffold is eaten rather than removed. Cellular Agriculture Ltd is one of its investors.還有一個新進者值得注意，因為它把問題反轉了。EdiMembre 於 2025 年 8 月自 MilliporeSigma 分拆，種子前輪 40 萬美元，其中空纖維由大豆、綠豆與扁豆蛋白分離物紡製而成，因此支架是被吃掉而不是被移除。Cellular Agriculture Ltd 是其投資人之一。

Five cultivated-meat companies stopped operating between mid-2024 and late 2025: SCiFi Foods, Upstream Foods, CellRev, Meatable and Believer Meats. Every stated reason was the same, an inability to raise. Sector investment peaked near $1 billion in 2021.2024 年年中到 2025 年年底，五家細胞培養肉公司停止營運：SCiFi Foods、Upstream Foods、CellRev、Meatable、Believer Meats。對外說法完全一致：募不到錢。該領域投資在 2021 年見頂，接近 10 億美元。
