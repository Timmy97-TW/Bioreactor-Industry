09 · Writing

What to put on the bioreactor page, and what to put on the homepage生物反應器頁面要寫什麼，首頁又要放什麼

The bioreactor page carries the argument. The homepage carries one sentence of it. They are not the same job, and the homepage sentence is the harder one.生物反應器頁面承載整段論證，首頁只承載其中一句。這是兩件不同的工作，而首頁那一句比較難。

The bioreactor page, in the order a judge reads it生物反應器頁面，依評審閱讀順序

  1. Open on the industry, not on ourselves. Fewer than one fermentation facility in five worldwide has a vessel above 20,000 L, and only nine have one above 100,000 L. The industry's shortage is of large vessels. Ours is a different shortage, and naming theirs first is what earns the right to name ours.從產業開場，不要從自己開場。全世界不到五分之一的發酵廠有 20,000 L 以上的槽體，只有九座超過 100,000 L。產業缺的是大槽。我們缺的是另一種東西，而先講清楚他們缺什麼，才換得到講我們缺什麼的資格。
  2. Then the architecture, with the reason it was chosen. Perfusion with full cell retention, single-use module, scale out by module count. Cite van Reis for the scale-up rule and say which similarity group is sacrificed. Dr. Yuet recommended a packed bed and we still owe him a written justification for hollow fibre. Put that on the page. It reads as engineering, not as an oversight.再講架構，並且講為什麼選它。全細胞截留的灌流、拋棄式模組、以模組數量橫向擴充。放大規則引用 van Reis，並說明放棄了哪一組相似性。Yuet 老師建議的是填充床，我們還欠他一份選擇中空纖維的書面理由。把這件事寫在頁面上。它讀起來是工程，不是疏漏。
  3. Then the measurement, and the failure inside it. 434 hours of unbroken logging. The scatter rose between 240 and 300 hours, and over exactly those hours the photometer's reference channel fell from 25.8 to 9.3 lux. The instrument recorded when to distrust itself. Lead with that, not with the growth curve.再講量測，以及量測裡的失效。434 小時不間斷紀錄。散布在第 240 至 300 小時之間放大，而正好在那幾個小時，光度計的參考通道從 25.8 lux 掉到 9.3 lux。儀器記錄下了何時不該相信自己。用這件事開頭，不要用生長曲線。
  4. Then the model, with its corrections visible. The critical-flux particle radius was wrong at 1.5 µm and is right at 0.7 µm, and correcting it moved the fouling margin from 0.26 to 0.73. Showing a correction is worth more than showing a result.再講模型，並且讓修正可見。臨界通量的粒徑取 1.5 µm 是錯的，正確值是 0.7 µm，修正後結垢餘裕從 0.26 移到 0.73。展示一次修正，比展示一個結果值錢。
  5. Then the limits, at the same type size as the claims. Every invariant is a model output. No biological output has been measured. Delivery stops at a reservoir. The membrane cutoff is unchosen. Contained use is a classification being pursued, not one held.再講限制，字級與主張相同。所有不變量都是模型輸出。沒有量測到任何生物產出。輸送止於儲槽。膜截留值未選定。封閉使用是正在爭取的分類，不是已經持有的分類。

Homepage highlight, three candidates首頁重點，三個候選

A

The empty quadrant空的象限

"Nine fermentation plants in the world have a vessel above 100,000 litres. None of them is within a kilometre of a field."「全世界有九座發酵廠擁有 10 萬公升以上的槽體。沒有任何一座在田的一公里之內。」

Strongest on evidence. It uses an industry number rather than a claim about ourselves, and the second sentence does all the work without asserting anything we have not built.證據面最強。用的是產業數字而不是關於自己的主張，而且第二句完成了全部工作，卻沒有主張任何我們尚未做出的東西。

B · recommended

Whether against how much有沒有，對上多少

"A sachet of spores answers whether. A reactor answers how much. Thursday looks hot, so we turn it up on Tuesday."「一包孢子只能回答有沒有。反應器回答的是多少。星期四看起來會很熱，所以我們星期二把量調上去。」

This is the project's own refusal of its cheapest competitor, and it is the only framing that survives contact with the not-yet-true list. It claims a capability of the architecture, not a result of an experiment.這是專案對自己最廉價競爭者的拒絕，也是唯一能在「尚未成立」清單前存活的框架。它主張的是架構的能力，不是實驗的結果。

C

Permission許可

"Nothing about the biology is proprietary. Everything between the fermenter and the field is."「生物學本身沒有一處是專利的。專利的是發酵槽與田之間的每一段。」

The truest sentence the project owns, and the hardest to follow with a demonstration. Use it as the closing line of the bioreactor page rather than as the homepage hook.專案擁有的最真實的一句，但也最難用展示接續。把它放在生物反應器頁面的結尾，而不是首頁的鉤子。

Delete before a judge sees it評審看到之前先刪掉

  • Any percentage cost reduction, and the words cheap, low-cost, affordable任何成本下降百分比，以及便宜、低成本、負擔得起這些字
  • "Farmers just add water and a carbon source"「農民只要加水和碳源」
  • Rice straw or any agricultural waste as feedstock稻稈或任何農業廢棄物作為原料
  • AI climate prediction described as a working feature. Forecast lead time is not on record, and the prediction argument only closes if lead time exceeds end-to-end latency把 AI 氣候預測寫成已運作的功能。預報前置時間沒有紀錄，而預測論證只有在前置時間大於端到端延遲時才成立
  • "Zero cold chain" written as achieved rather than as an argument about architecture把「零冷鏈」寫成已達成，而不是寫成關於架構的論證
  • The Nespresso comparison, and the word democratising雀巢膠囊機的比喻，以及「平民化」這個詞

House rules that apply to every line above上述每一行都適用的規則

No em dashes. Sentence case headings. No delve, leverage, robust, seamless, comprehensive, empower, harness, revolutionise. Vary sentence length; fragments are allowed. State the limit immediately after the claim it limits, at the same type size. Never infer from silence: a missing fact is not on record, and you may not write its negation either.不用破折號。標題用句子式大小寫。不用 delve、leverage、robust、seamless、comprehensive、empower、harness、revolutionise。句長要變化，允許不完整句。限制緊接在它所限制的主張之後，字級相同。不要從沉默推論：沒有的事實就是沒有紀錄，也不可以寫它的否定。

And on images: cleanrooms, banks of white coats and expensive instrumentation argue that you need a facility for this. Hand-built and slightly messy frames argue the opposite, which is our argument.關於影像：無塵室、成排的白袍與昂貴儀器，論證的是「你需要一座廠房才能做這件事」。手工做的、有點雜亂的畫面論證的是相反的事，而那才是我們的論證。
